# Waffle Brain Prompt Plan

## Goal

Create a high quality speaking activity bank.

The aim is not maximum quantity.

The aim is prompts that:
- feel fresh
- create natural conversation
- avoid boring ESL interview questions
- make teachers think "I wouldn't have thought of that"

---

# A1/A2 Beginner Prompt Bank

## Target

Students:
- can communicate basic ideas
- need support to speak longer
- benefit from examples and structure

The goal is not simple questions.

The goal is supported speaking activities.

Every prompt should contain:

1. A clear situation
2. Choices/examples where useful
3. A speaking path

Avoid:

- "Tell me about something..."
- "What do you think about..."
- abstract questions
- requiring the student to invent the topic

---

# Everyday Situations

## Purpose

Help students talk about familiar real-life topics.

## Prompt types

- daily routines
- home
- food
- hobbies
- travel
- family
- work/school

## Example style

"You are making a short video called 'My morning'. Tell me:

- What time do you wake up?
- What do you do first?
- What do you eat or drink?"

## Target prompts

30 prompts

---

# Describe

## Purpose

Build vocabulary and longer answers through concrete description.

## Prompt types

- objects
- places
- people
- pictures
- clothes
- food

## Example style

"Choose one object near you:

a phone
a book
a cup
a bag

Describe:

- What is it?
- What colour is it?
- Where is it?
- Why do you use it?"

## Target prompts

30 prompts

---

# Explain and Show

## Purpose

Practice simple explanations and sequencing.

## Prompt types

- instructions
- routines
- skills
- simple processes

## Example style

"Teach me how to make your favourite drink.

Tell me:

- What do we need?
- What do we do first?
- What do we do next?"

## Target prompts

30 prompts

# Simple Roleplay

## Purpose

Practice practical communication.

## Prompt types

- shops
- travel
- appointments
- asking for help

## Example style

"You are in a café.

You want to order food.

Tell me:

- What do you want?
- What questions do you ask?
- Do you have any problems?"

---

# Choose and Create

## Purpose

Help students make decisions and expand answers.

## Prompt types

- choosing between options
- creating simple plans
- designing ideas

## Example style

"You are opening a small café.

Choose three things for your menu:

pizza
sandwiches
cake
coffee

Explain:

- Why did you choose them?
- Who will buy them?"

## Target prompts

30 prompts

---

# Guided Stories

## Purpose

Practice storytelling without requiring the student to invent everything.

## Prompt types

- simple events
- sequences
- supported memories
- picture-style stories

## Example style

"Your day had a problem.

Choose one:

You missed the bus.
You lost your keys.
You forgot something important.

Tell me:

- What happened?
- What did you do?
- What happened next?"

## Target prompts

30 prompts

---

# A1/A2 Quality Check

Every prompt must:

✓ be understandable immediately

✓ create at least 1-2 minutes of speaking

✓ include support

✓ introduce useful vocabulary

✓ avoid one-word answers

✓ avoid abstract thinking

✓ avoid making the student invent the whole topic

The student should feel:

"I know how to start speaking."

# Prompt Quality Rules

Every prompt must:

✓ create speaking for at least 2 minutes

✓ have a clear task

✓ make the student do something

✓ avoid being a basic interview question

✓ be usable immediately by a teacher

---

Avoid prompts like:

"What is your favourite food?"

"Do you like travelling?"

"What is happiness?"

Instead:

"You are opening a restaurant. Create the menu and explain your choices."

"Plan your dream holiday for a friend."

"You have one week to become happier. What changes do you make?"
