# Waffle Brain A1/A2 Beginner Prompts

# Category: Everyday Situations

## Purpose

Create realistic everyday tasks where students use English to complete a goal, solve a small problem, or make a decision.

## Why students speak

Students speak because they need to achieve something, not because they are answering an interview question.

## Good prompt ingredients

- real life situations
- small problems
- decisions
- practical vocabulary
- simple choices when useful

Examples:
booking something
buying something
making plans
asking for help
solving a small mistake

## Prompt structure

Teacher:
Introduce the situation.

Student:
Completes the task.

Teacher:
Extends with follow-up questions.

## Avoid

- What is your favourite...
- Tell me about your day
- Simple preference questions
- Situations with no clear goal

## Language focus

Present simple
Present continuous
Going to future
Simple reasons
Practical vocabulary

## Design rule

These prompts should:

- use familiar real-life situations
- help students talk about daily life
- provide a clear starting point
- encourage useful vocabulary
- avoid interview-style questions

Students should talk about:

- routines
- home
- food
- shopping
- travel
- hobbies
- daily decisions

Avoid:

- "Tell me about your life"
- "What do you usually do?"
- questions with no structure

---
## Additional Design Rule

A good Everyday Situation prompt should not only ask the student to choose.

The student should have a reason for choosing.

Every activity should include:

- a clear situation
- a goal
- a small problem or decision
- a reason to explain their choice

The teacher should be able to naturally continue the conversation.

The activity should create interaction, not just answers.

# Approved Prompt Bank
## 1. Plan a Busy Saturday Visitor

Teacher setup:

Tell the student:

"Your friend is visiting your town on Saturday. They like food and outdoor activities, but they don't like museums."

Student task:

Create a plan for the day.

Choose:

- morning activity
- lunch place
- afternoon activity

Explain:

- where you go
- what you do
- why your friend will like it

Teacher extension:

Ask:
"What happens if it rains?"
"Can you make the plan cheaper?"


Language focus:

Going to future
Present simple
Reasons
