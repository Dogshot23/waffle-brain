# Waffle Brain A1/A2 Category System

## Everyday Situations

Purpose:
Real life tasks and small problems.

Students:
make decisions, complete tasks, solve simple situations.


## Describe

Purpose:
Help students describe familiar things.

Students:
give details using structure.


## Explain and Show

Purpose:
Help students explain processes and routines.

Students:
teach, demonstrate, explain.


## Choose and Create

Purpose:
Help students make decisions and build something.

Students:
compare options and justify choices.


## Guided Stories

Purpose:
Help students create short stories with support.

Students:
build a story using structure.


## Simple Roleplay

Purpose:
Practise functional conversations.

Students:
communicate to achieve a goal.
