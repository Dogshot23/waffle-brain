# Waffle Brain A1/A2 Beginner Prompts

# Category: Guided Stories

## Category Purpose

Guided Stories helps beginners create short stories using structure and support.

Students should not need to invent a complete story.

The prompt provides a simple situation, choices, and a clear sequence.

The goal is confidence with speaking, sequencing, and basic storytelling language.

The student should feel guided through the story rather than responsible for inventing the entire narrative.

---

## Design Rule

Every prompt should include:

- a clear situation
- a simple story goal
- choices or support
- a beginning, middle and ending

Students should be able to use:

First...

Then...

After that...

Finally...

---

## Good Examples

Good:

"Your friend visits your town for one day.

Plan the story:

First:
where you go

Then:
what happens

Finally:
how the day ends"

Good:

"You lose something important.

Create the story:

What do you lose?

Where do you look?

Who helps you?"

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- "Tell me about a time when..."
- personal memories requiring long answers
- emotional or abstract stories
- complex characters
- fantasy worlds

Avoid:

"Tell a story about your life."

---

## Language Focus

Main language:

- First...
- Then...
- After that...
- Finally...
- I went...
- I saw...
- I found...
- We decided...

---

## Prompt Structure

Situation

↓

Story problem or goal

↓

Choices/support

↓

Story sequence

↓

Teacher extension questions

---

## Quality Check

Ask:

Can a beginner start immediately?

Is the story supported?

Can the student speak for 1–3 minutes?

Does it feel like creating a story rather than answering questions?

## Activity Templates

Every Guided Stories prompt should use one of these structures.

---

### Template 1 — Small Problem Story

The student creates a story around a simple problem.

Examples:

lost item

late arrival

broken object

forgot something

Structure:

Beginning:

What happens?

Middle:

What problem appears?

Ending:

How is it solved?

---

### Template 2 — A Day Story

The student creates a short story about a day.

Examples:

special day

busy day

unexpected day

trip day

Structure:

First:

What happens?

Then:

What do they do?

Finally:

How does the day finish?

---

### Template 3 — Choose the Story Path

The student chooses options that change the story.

Examples:

place

person

problem

activity

Structure:

Choose:

• where it happens
• who is involved
• what happens

Create the story.

---

### Template 4 — Picture in Your Mind

The teacher creates a simple scene and the student builds the story.

Examples:

a park

a shop

a street

a house

Structure:

Describe:

• where they are
• what they see
• what happens next

---

### Template 5 — Help Someone Story

The student creates a story where someone needs help.

Examples:

friend

family member

visitor

new student

Structure:

Who needs help?

What is the problem?

What happens?

---

### Template 6 — Before and After

The student describes a change.

Examples:

room before and after cleaning

before and after a trip

before and after learning something

Structure:

Before:

What was it like?

Change:

What happened?

After:

What is different?
