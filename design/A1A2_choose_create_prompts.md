# Waffle Brain A1/A2 Beginner Prompts

# Category: Choose and Create

## Category Purpose

Choose and Create helps beginners practise making simple decisions and creating small plans.

Students do not need to invent ideas from nothing.

They choose from clear options and build something using familiar language.

---

## Design Rule

Every prompt should include:

- a clear goal
- limited choices
- a simple creation task
- reasons for choices

The student should create something real or realistic.

Examples:

a plan

a menu

a schedule

a shopping list

a trip idea

---

## Good Examples

Good:

"Create a weekend plan.

Choose:

place:
park
city
beach

activity:
walking
shopping
eating out

Explain:

what you choose
why you choose it"

Good:

"Create a breakfast menu for a small café."

Choose:

food
drinks
prices

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- unlimited imagination
- fantasy worlds
- impossible situations
- "If you could..."
- dream scenarios

Avoid:

"Create your perfect life."

This requires abstract language.

---

## Language Focus

Main language:

- I choose...
- I want...
- I prefer...
- We can...
- Let's...
- I think...
- because...

---

## Prompt Structure

Goal

↓

Choices/options

↓

Create something

↓

Explain choices

↓

Teacher extension questions

---

## Quality Check

Ask:

Does the student have enough support?

Are choices provided?

Can a beginner explain their decisions?

Would this create natural conversation?

## Activity Templates

Every Choose and Create prompt should use one of these structures.

---

### Template 1 — Build a Plan

The student creates a simple plan using choices.

Examples:

weekend plan

day out

holiday

party

school day

Structure:

Choose:

• place
• activity
• time
• people

Create the plan and explain choices.

---

### Template 2 — Make a Menu

The student creates a simple food or drink selection.

Examples:

restaurant menu

party food

breakfast

picnic

Structure:

Choose:

• food
• drinks
• extras

Explain:

what you choose

why people will like it

---

### Template 3 — Pack and Prepare

The student chooses items for a situation.

Examples:

trip

school day

sport activity

moving house

Structure:

Choose:

• important items
• optional items
• reasons

---

### Template 4 — Design a Space

The student creates a simple place.

Examples:

bedroom

study area

shop

café

Structure:

Choose:

• things to include
• where things go
• why they are useful

---

### Template 5 — Choose Between Options

The student compares choices and makes a decision.

Examples:

two hotels

two activities

two products

Structure:

Choose:

• option A or B
• reasons
• final decision

---

### Template 6 — Create Something for Someone

The student solves a simple need.

Examples:

gift

plan for a friend

activity for family

help for a visitor

Structure:

Choose:

• what to create
• who it is for
• why it works
