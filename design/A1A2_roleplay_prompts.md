# Waffle Brain A1/A2 Beginner Prompts

# Category: Simple Roleplay

## Purpose

Practise useful real conversations.

## Why students speak

Students have a clear role and reason to communicate.

## Good prompt ingredients

- realistic situations
- clear roles
- simple goals

Examples:

restaurant
hotel
shop
doctor
travel

## Prompt structure

Teacher:
Sets the situation.

Student:
Uses language to complete the interaction.

Teacher:
Responds naturally.

## Avoid

- complicated characters
- acting games
- unrealistic scenarios

## Language focus

Requests
Questions
Polite phrases
Functional vocabulary

## Design rule

These prompts should:

- create a real communication situation
- give the student a clear goal
- provide useful vocabulary
- create interaction, not memorised dialogue

The student should have:

Situation:
What is happening?

Goal:
What do they need to do?

Support:
Useful ideas to say.

Avoid:

- complicated problems
- angry complaints
- business situations
- abstract negotiation

---

# 1

You are in a café.

You want to order lunch.

Choose:

sandwich
pizza
salad
cake

Tell the waiter:

- What you want
- What you would like to drink
- Ask one question

---

# 2

You are staying in a hotel.

There is a problem with your room.

Choose:

the TV does not work
the room is too cold
you need a different room

Tell the receptionist:

- Your problem
- What you need
- Say thank you

---

# 3

You are buying clothes.

You want to buy something new.

Choose:

jacket
shoes
shirt
trousers

Ask about:

- Price
- Size
- Colour

---

# 4

You are at a train station.

You need to travel somewhere.

Choose:

another city
the airport
a tourist place

Ask:

- What time is the train?
- Where is the platform?
- How much is the ticket?

---

# 5

You are meeting a new neighbour.

Introduce yourself.

Tell them:

- Your name
- Where you live
- Something about yourself

Ask:

- Their name
- A question about them

---

# 6

You are inviting a friend somewhere.

Choose:

cinema
restaurant
park
sports event

Tell your friend:

- Where you want to go
- When
- Why it is fun

---

# 7

You are lost in a new city.

Ask someone for help.

Tell them:

- Where you want to go
- What you are looking for

Ask:

- Where is it?
- How can I get there?

---

# 8

You bought food but there is a problem.

Choose:

wrong food
missing item
cold food

Explain:

- What happened
- What you want

---

# 9

You are making a phone call.

You need information.

Choose:

doctor appointment
restaurant booking
class information

Ask:

- What you need to know
- When you want it

---

# 10

You are returning something to a shop.

Choose:

wrong size
wrong colour
broken item

Explain:

- What happened
- What you would like

---

# 11

You are at a friend's house.

You want to learn about their city.

Ask about:

- Places to visit
- Food
- Things to do

---

# 12

You are planning a day with a friend.

Choose:

beach day
city trip
home day
sports day

Decide:

- Where to go
- What to do
- What to bring

---

# 13

You are joining a new class.

Introduce yourself.

Tell people:

- Your name
- Your interests
- Why you joined

---

# 14

You need to make an appointment.

Choose:

doctor
hairdresser
teacher

Tell them:

- When you want to come
- What you need

---

# 15

You are helping a tourist.

They ask you about your town.

Recommend:

- One place
- One food
- One activity

Explain why.

---

# Review

Language focus:

Functional English:

"I would like..."
"Can I have..."
"Where is..."
"Could you help me?"

Grammar:

Present simple
Basic questions
Can / need / want

Goal:

The student has a reason to communicate, not just answer questions.
