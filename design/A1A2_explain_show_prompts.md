# Category: Explain and Show

## Category Purpose

Explain and Show helps beginners practise explaining simple processes, instructions and routines.

The student should feel like they are teaching or showing something to the teacher.

The goal is not a perfect explanation.

The goal is clear communication using simple steps.

---

## Design Rule

Every prompt should include:

- something concrete to explain or show
- a clear sequence
- simple steps
- useful everyday language

Students should be able to use:

First...
Then...
Next...
Finally...

---

## Good Examples

Good:

"Show your teacher how you make breakfast."

Talk about:

- what you need
- first step
- next steps
- final result

Good:

"Explain how to travel to your home."

Talk about:

- starting point
- transport
- important places
- final destination

---

## Avoid

Avoid:

- abstract advice
- opinions
- life philosophy
- complicated instructions

Avoid:

"Explain how to become successful."

This is not beginner communication.

---

## Language Focus

Main language:

- First...
- Then...
- After that...
- Finally...
- You need...
- You can...
- I usually...

---

# Review

Language focus:

Present simple:
"I usually..."
"You need..."

Sequencing:
"First..."
"Then..."
"Finally..."

Vocabulary:
- routines
- food
- transport
- hobbies

Goal:

The student should be able to speak for 1-3 minutes with support.

## Activity Templates

Every Explain and Show prompt should use one of these structures.

---

### Template 1 — Show Me How

The student teaches the teacher how to do a simple everyday action.

Examples:

making food

using an app

getting ready

doing a hobby

Structure:

Show:

• what you need
• first step
• next steps
• final result

---

### Template 2 — Give Directions

The student explains how to get somewhere.

Examples:

home

school

work

a local place

Structure:

Explain:

• starting point
• transport
• important places
• arrival

---

### Template 3 — Explain Your System

The student explains how they organise something.

Examples:

bag

desk

room

schedule

phone

Structure:

Show:

• what you organise
• how you organise it
• why you do it this way

---

### Template 4 — Teach a Skill

The student is the expert.

Examples:

sport

game

hobby

simple activity

Structure:

Explain:

• what beginners need
• first thing to do
• common mistakes
• advice

---

### Template 5 — Help a Beginner

The teacher pretends they do not know something.

The student explains it simply.

Examples:

how to use public transport

how to order food

how to prepare for a trip

Structure:

Explain:

• what to do first
• what to remember
• useful tips

---

### Template 6 — Prepare Together

The student explains how they prepare for something.

Examples:

trip

party

school day

special event

Structure:

Talk about:

• what you need
• what you do first
• what happens next
